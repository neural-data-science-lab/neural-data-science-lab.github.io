Simon's research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and cognitive neuroscience, with a focus on
explainable AI (XAI) and representational alignment between deep learning models and humans.
This work combines naturalistic paradigms, such as immersive VR and 3D models, with large-scale data from
population-based health studies and online experiments.
Simon also develops open-source research software, including [`scilaunch`](https://shescher.github.io/scilaunch/), and `xai4mri`.

Simon studied philosophy and psychology in Berlin and computational cognitive science at the University of Amsterdam.
The doctoral research, carried out at MPI CBS and the Machine Learning Department of TU Berlin in collaboration with
Fraunhofer HHI, applied XAI methods to neuroimaging and behavorial data.

At MPI CBS, Simon founded [CBS CoCoNUT](https://www.cbs.mpg.de/en/cbs-coconut), an open research forum with talks, workshops and journal clubs.
Outside academia, Simon has a long background in film and has worked for the Berlinale since 2013,
including as a member of the selection committee for Berlinale Generation, author and moderator.
